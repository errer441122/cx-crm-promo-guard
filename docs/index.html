Hold → Cooldown → Automatic Release Lifecycle

Suppressing unhappy buyers is only half the job. Without the companion daily release workflow, contacts stay excluded forever. Every hold follows four clear states:

State 1

Eligible (Normal)

Customer receives standard newsletters, promos, and post-purchase review invitations.

State 2 · Promo Guard

Active Promo Hold

Stuck parcel, 1–2★ review, or open ticket immediately sets cx_promo_hold = true & pauses review flows.

State 3 · Buffer

Post-Fix Cooldown

Even after delivery or ticket resolution, contact stays protected for 7 days (late parcel) or 30 days (1–2★ review).

State 4 · 07:30 Job

Automatic Release

Daily 07:30 CET job verifies zero open tickets + expired cooldown and restores cx_promo_hold = false cleanly.

4 Human-in-the-Loop Guardrails Built Into the Workflow

Customer service is high-trust. Every AI step is bounded by hard workflow and prompt rules:

Zero Auto-Replies

Drafts Are Private Internal Notes Only

The workflow posts the AI empathy draft strictly as a yellow Zendesk Internal Note. A human agent always reviews, edits, and sends the final email.

Margin & Policy Safe

No Unauthorized Refunds or Coupons

The prompt explicitly forbids promising money back, reshipments, or discount vouchers — the draft only acknowledges the issue and next step.

Instant Escalation

Chargeback & Legal Threats Force P0

Any mention of chargeback, bank dispute, or legal action overrides standard LTV scoring and fires an immediate Slack alert to #cx-vip-alerts.

Identity Stitching

Resolves Missing Emails via Shopify

Carrier webhooks and Trustpilot exports often carry only an order_id. Shopify lookup resolves the email and lifetime spend automatically.
