    What the AI is not allowed to do

    Write to the customer.

    The draft goes into the Zendesk ticket as a private internal note. An agent edits it and decides whether to send it.

    Promise a refund, a replacement or a discount.

    The triage prompt forbids it. Only a person can authorise compensation.

    Let a chargeback threat wait in the queue.

    The prompt sets P0 on words like chargeback, PayPal dispute, lawyer or fraud, and only P0 posts to #cx-vip-alerts.

    Keep a customer on hold forever.

    The release job runs every morning, so a resolved customer goes back into the normal flows instead of shrinking the list.
