e-commerce customer care + email marketing · n8n + Klaviyo

Stop sending promo emails to angry customers waiting on a problem.

If a parcel gets stuck, a buyer leaves a 1-star review, or opens a support ticket, this automation pauses discounts and review requests in Klaviyo—and prepares a private reply draft for your support agent.

02 · marketing paused + safe draftProtects the customer relationship

Sets an automatic pause date in Klaviyo so no promo or review request goes out, and writes a private draft in Zendesk (never auto-sent).

03 · VIP alert + auto-resumeFast rescue, zero forgotten blocks

VIP buyers or chargeback threats trigger an instant Slack alert; once the pause date expires, the customer re-enters normal flows automatically.

      What the AI is never allowed to do

      Email the customer directly.

      The reply draft is saved in Zendesk strictly as a private internal note. A human agent always reviews, edits and sends it.

      Promise a refund, replacement or discount.

      The prompt explicitly forbids monetary promises. Only a human support agent can authorise compensation.

      Let a chargeback threat wait in line.

      Words like chargeback, bank dispute, lawyer or fraud automatically force priority P0 and ping #cx-vip-alerts immediately.

      Decide how long the pause lasts.

      Fixed deterministic code rules set every pause window (7 / 14 / 30 days) with an explicit end date so nobody stays blocked forever.
